How EasyInvite Prevents Emails from Going to Spam

100%

Optimal Inbox Placement

Emails dispatched via EasyInvite are engineered to pass Google, Yahoo, and Microsoft spam filters with maximum trust.

✓
Authenticated via Gmail SMTP (SPF & DKIM Pass 100%)

Because you send directly through your Google Gmail account, Gmail automatically cryptographically signs every message with genuine Google DKIM (d=gmail.com). It is never sent through third-party unverified relay IPs.

✓
Multipart MIME (HTML + Clean Plain-Text Alternative)

Spam filters severely penalize HTML-only emails. EasyInvite automatically generates a synchronous plain-text version alongside the styled HTML message, eliminating the MIME_HTML_ONLY spam penalty.

✓
Whitelisted Official Google Domains

Your invitations link directly to official play.google.com testing tracks. Google’s algorithms recognize their own domain and treat it as trusted, unlike shortened links (bit.ly, etc.).

✓
Personal Direct Dispatch (Zero Disclosed CC/BCC Lists)

Each tester receives an individual 1-on-1 email with Precedence: personal headers. There are no bulk CC lists or commercial headers that trigger the "Promotions" or "Junk" tab.

✓
Staggered Pacing (Anti-Burst Throttle Protection)

Emails are dispatched with a natural pacing delay between recipients, preventing Google’s automated heuristic triggers from flagging sudden robotic bursts.

✓
CAN-SPAM Compliant Legitimate Opt-Out Notice

Every outgoing email includes a clean footer explaining why the recipient received the invitation and clarifying that they can simply ignore it if they don't wish to test.

💡 Developer Tip for New Tester Lists:

If you are emailing someone for the very first time, ask them to check their Updates or Spam folder once and tap “Report not spam” or “Move to Primary”. Once marked, all your future testing invitations and updates will arrive directly in their Primary Inbox!
